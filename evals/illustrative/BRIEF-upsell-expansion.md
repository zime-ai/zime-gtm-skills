minute (usage dropped after the admin who ran the account left), and the
champion's advocacy is only ever personal enthusiasm ("I'm really excited
about it") with no evidence of internal standing or a stated intent to
push it through approval. Every other dimension (concrete ask, strategic
alignment, open issue named, enablement-gap read, clear next step) is
genuinely strong.

**Planted traps:**
1. The rubric's "Do not" section says don't score the other seven
   dimensions as strong enough to offset a genuine account-health
   risk — dimension 1 gates the read, it doesn't average with the rest. A
   skill whose closing read is "Go" because six of eight dimensions are
   Covered, rather than "Hold" because dimension 1 surfaced real risk,
   fails this trap.
2. The rubric's "Do not" section says don't infer champion strength from
   personal enthusiasm alone — score it only from evidence of internal
   standing or a stated intent to advocate. Sam's "I'm really excited" is
   exactly that failure case. A skill that scores dimension 3 Covered off
   Sam's enthusiasm, rather than Missed for lacking any stated advocacy
   plan, fails this trap.

(usage drop tied to the departed admin) — flagged plainly before the rest
is scored, per the skill's own instructions. Covered on expansion ask
concreteness, strategic alignment, open risk named (export bug), and

concrete signal like budget). Close: **Hold**, not Go — the health risk
from dimension 1 gates the read regardless of how strong the rest of the
pitch looks (trap 1).
