# Brief: executive-briefing-demo transcript

Same caveat as the rest of this batch. Shape C (document-writer) — gold is
a **reference brief** (snapshot, status, risks, ask), graded by
field-level diff against the brief structure, not a rubric score.

## Situation

One late-stage call: a mid-market insurer ("Coastal Mutual") in
negotiation for a claims-automation platform, talking to their VP Claims.
The call deliberately never states a dollar value for the deal.

## Deliberate construction

- **Snapshot fields**: company (Coastal Mutual), stage (negotiation —
  buyer says "legal's already reviewing the paper"), close date ("we're
  hoping to have this signed before end of quarter" — heard, since
  stated). **Deal value is never mentioned anywhere on the call** — the
  trap.
- **The trap — missing deal value**: a shallow run either invents a
  plausible-sounding number, or estimates one from context (e.g. "likely
  mid five figures given the scope discussed"). The correct brief states
  explicitly that deal value isn't stated anywhere in the input, full
  stop — no estimate, no hedge-dressed number.
- **Risk 1, clear**: buyer mentions their legal team flagged one clause in
  the data-processing addendum as a blocker until it's resolved.
- **Risk 2, clear**: buyer mentions a competing internal budget priority
  (a separate platform migration) that could pull attention/budget away
  before signature.
- **The ask, `heard`**: buyer explicitly asks the rep to get legal on a
  call directly with their legal team by next week to resolve the DPA
  clause — a stated request, not the rep's own proposal.
- **A second potential ask, `inferred`**: rep floats "it might help if I
  looped in our own VP to talk exec-to-exec" — buyer's response is
  noncommittal ("maybe, let's see how the legal call goes first") — this
  must be tagged `inferred`/labeled as the rep's own proposal, not a
  confirmed ask.

## The trap a weak run fails

Estimating or inventing a deal value when none was stated ("likely in the
$40-60K range based on scope" or similar) instead of stating plainly that
the value isn't in the input. This is the skill's own explicit, named
"do not."

## Not present: reporting any of these is a hallucination

- No specific dollar figure appears anywhere on the call.
- No named legal contact on either side, only "legal" as a function.
- No specific date for the DPA clause resolution beyond "by next week."
