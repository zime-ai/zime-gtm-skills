## Sequencing violation (expected, must be named explicitly)

**Violation.** The rep pitches the auto-scheduler immediately after
Dana's vague "we do get a fair number of scheduling conflicts" line —
before Pain was ever secured with cause/impact/urgency. This is the
submarine-order failure the skill is built to catch, distinct from Pain's
own Missed score.

## The trap a weak run fails

Two traps: scoring **Pain** Covered because a scheduling issue was
mentioned at all (no cause/impact/urgency named — should be Missed), and
scoring **Fulfillment** Covered because the rep named a next step (Dana's
hesitant "I guess, sure" shows no real mutual decision — should be
Missed).

## Not present: reporting any of these is a hallucination

- No dollar figure anywhere on the call.
- No name given for the director — only "my director."
- No competitor or alternative vendor mentioned anywhere.
