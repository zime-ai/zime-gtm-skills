## The trap a weak run fails

Estimating or inventing a deal value ("likely $40-60K based on scope")
instead of stating plainly that no value is given anywhere in the input.
The skill's own rule forbids this explicitly.

## Not present: reporting any of these is a hallucination

- No dollar figure anywhere on the call.
- No named legal contact on either side, only "legal" as a function.
- No specific date for DPA resolution beyond "by next week."
