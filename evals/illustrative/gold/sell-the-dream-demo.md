## Sequencing (expected, must be named explicitly)

**Sequencing violation.** Element 4 (the feature pitch) is the rep's
opening line — before any of elements 1-3 are established. Elements 1-3
only appear afterward, backfilled to sound strategic. Even though every
element is individually present somewhere in the call, this is exactly
the pattern the skill exists to catch: a rep who pitched product first
and added vision framing after the fact, not a call that built the vision
before introducing the product.

## The trap a weak run fails

Scoring all five elements Covered/Partial based on presence alone and
concluding "no violation found" because nothing is technically missing.
The correct read requires checking *order*, not just presence — elements
1-3 landing after element 4 is the violation, regardless of each
element's individual score.
