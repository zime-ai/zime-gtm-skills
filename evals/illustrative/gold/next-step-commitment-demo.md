## Expected verdict

**Weak commitment** (a defensible case also exists for **No commitment**,
since nothing but scheduling another call was agreed to — either verdict
should be accepted if the reasoning below is present; the failure mode
this brief targets is scoring it **Real**).

## What was said

"Should we grab time next week to keep this moving?" / "Sure, works for
me."

## Missing (expected)

- **No specific action** — "keep this moving" names no concrete
  deliverable, review, or decision.
- **No exact date or trigger** — "next week" is a rough window, not a
  calendar date or checkable event.
- **One-sided** — only the rep proposes the next call; the buyer's "sure,
  works for me" is agreement to be scheduled, not a two-sided commitment
  to do anything.

## The trap a weak run fails

Scoring this **Real commitment** because "next week" and "keep this
moving" sound forward-moving together, without checking that none of the
three required elements (specific action, exact date/trigger, two-sided
ownership) are actually present in checkable form.

## Not present: reporting any of these is a hallucination

- No specific calendar date stated anywhere — only "next week."
- No named deliverable (proposal, contract, review) in the closing
  exchange.
- No third participant — only the rep and the Ops Director speak.
