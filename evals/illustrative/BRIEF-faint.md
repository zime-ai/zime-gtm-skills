# Brief: faint-demo transcript

Same caveat as every run in this batch. Shape A (findings-recall): gold is
a **ranked five-criterion scorecard**, graded by per-criterion agreement
and a hallucination check.

## Situation

Outbound discovery call: a mid-market e-commerce company ("Larkspur
Goods") that responded to a cold campaign, no stated problem yet, buyer is
curious but hasn't named a need.

## Deliberate construction

- **Funds — Covered**: buyer says general financial capacity is there —
  "we're not tight on cash right now, if this was worth it we could
  probably just fold it into next quarter's ops tooling budget."
- **Authority — the trap, should be Partial not Covered**: buyer is
  titled "Ops Lead" and speaks confidently, but the only thing said about
  decision path is "I'd bring it to my director if we ever got serious." A
  shallow run scores Authority Covered from the confident tone or the
  title alone; the rubric explicitly forbids inferring Authority from
  title, and the transcript only shows an influencer path, not final say.
- **Interest — Covered**: buyer asks several genuine follow-up questions
  about how the product works and asks to see a demo — real curiosity,
  unprompted.
- **Need — genuinely not surfaced, must not be treated as a disqualifying
  miss**: buyer never names a specific problem, explicitly says "we
  haven't really identified a specific pain point, just curious what's out
  there." The correct read frames this as "not yet surfaced," not a
  disqualifying Missed, since Interest is genuinely Covered — that's
  FAINT's whole premise.
- **Timing — Missed**: no trigger event, deadline, or timeline is
  discussed anywhere.

## The trap a weak run fails

Two traps: (1) inferring Authority as Covered from the "Ops Lead" title or
confident tone, when the only decision-path evidence shown is an
influencer relationship to a director — should be Partial; (2) letting the
un-surfaced Need pull the overall advance/no-advance call down as if it
were a disqualifying miss, instead of correctly reading it as "not yet
surfaced" given genuinely strong Interest.

## Not present: reporting any of these is a hallucination

- No specific dollar figure or budget number is stated anywhere on the
  call.
- No named director or other stakeholder beyond the buyer themselves.
- No trigger event or deadline of any kind is mentioned.
