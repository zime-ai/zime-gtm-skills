# Brief: pilot-to-conversion illustrative eval

**Transcript:** `transcripts/pilot-to-conversion-demo.txt` — Fenwick
Insurance mid-pilot check-in, rep Robin, Claims Ops Lead contact.

**Scenario design.** Most dimensions are genuinely strong and specific
(plan, integration, setup, feature feedback, results). The rep closes with
plain optimism ("I'm feeling really good... I think we're in great shape")
but never names a specific next step, date, or owner for conversion.

**Planted trap:** the rubric's "Do not" section says don't infer conversion
likelihood from rep optimism — score dimension 8 only from a specific named
next step, date, and owner. A skill that scores dimension 8 (clear
conversion path) as Covered or Partial off Robin's optimistic close,
instead of Missed for lacking any named date/owner, fails this trap.

**Expected gold shape:** Covered on plan/criteria, technical integration,
setup experience, feature feedback, and results-vs-criteria (specific
1.8%-vs-2% figure). Partial on admin/security readiness (9 of 12
provisioned) and user engagement (specific figure given, but declining and
unexplained — the rubric's "Covered" bar is a specific figure, which this
has, so Covered is also defensible; either is fine as long as the decline
is flagged). Missed on conversion path (trap). Close: on-track or at-risk,
not "stalled" — but explicitly flag the missing conversion path and the
usage dip as the risk, not glossed over by the rep's optimism.
