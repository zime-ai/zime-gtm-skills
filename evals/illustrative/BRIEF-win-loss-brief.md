## Situation

Final call on a lost deal: a mid-market events company ("Pallister
Events") explaining to the rep why they went with a competitor instead of
signing, after a long evaluation.

## Deliberate construction — outcome: lost

- **Driver 1, clear, competitive displacement**: buyer states plainly they
  signed with "Vantage" because Vantage's onboarding team could start two
  weeks sooner.
- **Driver 2, clear, champion strength/loss**: buyer's internal champion
  left the company mid-evaluation, and no one else picked up the internal
  push — buyer says this directly ("once Renee left, honestly nobody else
  was carrying it internally").
- **Driver 3, clear, pricing/procurement friction**: buyer states their
  procurement team required a security audit that added six weeks, which
  the competitor's smaller deal size didn't trigger.
- **The trap — a hedge that must stay `tentative`**: buyer says "I don't
  know, maybe if we'd started the technical eval earlier it would've felt
  less rushed at the end" — a guess about a counterfactual, not a stated
  driver. A shallow run upgrades this to a clear "timing" driver
  ("evaluation started too late"); it must stay `tentative`, quoted as a
  hedge, not promoted to a confident cause.
- **Driver 4 candidate that should NOT appear as a driver at all**: buyer
  mentions the rep's product demo was well received — positive feedback,
  not a loss driver, must not be forced into the brief as a driver just to
  round out the list.

## The trap a weak run fails

Promoting "maybe if we'd started the technical eval earlier" into a
confident "timing" driver. The buyer explicitly hedges it ("I don't
know, maybe") — it belongs in the brief as `tentative`, if included at
all, not upgraded to sit alongside the three clear drivers.

## Not present: reporting any of these is a hallucination

- No specific dollar figure or contract value is stated on the call —
  none should be invented.
- The champion's name is Renee only — no surname given, no other champion
  should be invented.
- No specific week/date is given for when Renee left, only "mid-evaluation"
  — no exact date should be invented.
