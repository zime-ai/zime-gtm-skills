# Brief: first-call-rampup illustrative eval

**Transcript:** `transcripts/first-call-rampup-demo.txt` — Anders Foods
first call, new rep Sam, Ops Manager Jamie.

**Scenario design.** A genuinely strong first call by a new rep — open
questioning, pain uncovering, and trigger are all handled well, and rapport
is strong — with one clean miss (no framing at the very start) and one
generic-with-one-callback pitch.

**Planted trap:** the rubric's "Do not" section says don't let a wall of
Missed items stand if the reads-well-too check flags it as uniformly
harsh — and the skill scores dimensions independently rather than letting
one weak dimension color the rest. A skill that lets the Missed opening
bleed into harsher scoring elsewhere (e.g. marking rapport or pain
uncovering Partial "because the call didn't start well"), instead of
scoring each dimension strictly on its own evidence and closing with a
fair, motivating ratio, fails this trap.

**Expected gold shape:** Missed on strong opening (straight into "tell me
about your company" with no framing). Covered on open-ended questioning,
pain uncovered, trigger surfaced, and rapport. Partial on pitch clarity
(mostly generic, one specific callback to the penalty-clause trigger).
Close: exactly one coaching note — work on opening framing — not a list,
and not colored by the rest of the call being strong.
