# Brief: deal-highlights-demo transcript

Same caveat as the rest of this batch. Shape C (document-writer) — gold is
a **reference highlights list**, graded by item-recall/item-precision
against the skill's own six categories, not a rubric score.

## Situation

One mid-stage call: a healthcare SaaS buyer ("Solace Health") talking to
a rep about a claims-processing tool, with a good chunk of pure rapport
chatter mixed in with the substance.

## Deliberate construction — six categories, one deliberate non-highlight

- **Commitment**: buyer commits to sending last quarter's claim-volume
  numbers by Thursday.
- **Objection**: buyer says the price is "hard to justify without seeing
  ROI modeled against our own numbers first."
- **Buying signal**: buyer asks whether the tool can be piloted with one
  team before a wider rollout — an implementation-planning question, a
  real signal.
- **Competitive mention**: buyer says they're "also taking a look at
  Vantage," a named competitor.
- **Risk**: buyer mentions their current contract with a different vendor
  doesn't expire for five more months, which "might slow down how fast we
  can actually move."
- **Number that matters**: buyer states their team processes "about 40,000
  claims a month."
- **The trap — small talk that must NOT become a highlight**: an extended
  exchange about the rep's dog and the buyer's recent vacation, plus
  scheduling back-and-forth about which day works for a follow-up call. A
  shallow run pads the list with "buyer and rep built rapport discussing
  personal topics" as a highlight — it isn't one, none of the six
  categories cover generic rapport-building or scheduling chatter, per the
  skill's own rule.

## The trap a weak run fails

Treating the dog/vacation small talk or the call-scheduling exchange as a
highlight (e.g., "positive rapport built" or "next call scheduled for
Monday" phrased as if scheduling itself were a commitment). Only a
genuine commitment, objection, buying signal, competitive mention, risk,
or a number that matters earns a line — six real ones exist here, no more
are needed and none should be manufactured from the chatter.

## Not present: reporting any of these is a hallucination

- No specific dollar figure or contract value is stated anywhere on the
  call — none should be invented.
- No named individual beyond the rep and the buyer (referred to by role,
  no other name given) — no other contact should be invented.
- The competitor named is "Vantage" only — no other competitor name should
  appear.
