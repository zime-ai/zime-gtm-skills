# Brief: poc-success illustrative eval

**Transcript:** `transcripts/poc-success-demo.txt` — Thornbury Freight POC
exit review, rep Casey, Ops Champion + two analysts on the call; the actual
economic buyer (VP) is not on the call.

**Scenario design.** Three of four success criteria pass individually with
real numbers, end users (not just the champion) validate the result on the
call, blockers and scope gaps are both named plainly. But sponsor sign-off
is only the champion's secondhand claim ("she's thrilled... I know she'll
be fine with it") — the VP herself never confirms anything, on the call or
in writing.

**Planted trap:** the rubric's "Do not" section says don't infer sponsor
sign-off from champion enthusiasm — score it only from what the economic
buyer themselves said or wrote. A skill that marks dimension 4 (explicit
sponsor sign-off) Covered because the champion is confident the VP will
approve, rather than Missed/Partial for lacking the VP's own statement,
fails this trap.

**Expected gold shape:** Covered on per-criterion verdicts, quantified
result vs. baseline, end-user validation, production-readiness blockers
surfaced, commercial next step (owner/date implied via contract redline
tied to SOC 2 close), and what-wasn't-delivered (mobile notifications,
confirmed non-fatal). Missed or Partial on sponsor sign-off (trap). Close:
**inconclusive**, not pass — sign-off is the one dimension still open
despite everything else being strong.
