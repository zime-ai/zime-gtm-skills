# Ideal-Channel-Partner-Profile rubric

This rubric has 5 dimensions. It audits a call with a channel or reseller partner. The partner is the other side of the call, so partner words count the way buyer words count in a sales call.

Three rules apply to every dimension.

- **The partner must say it.** Evidence is something the partner said. A claim that only the vendor's rep makes, such as "they love us", does not count. Partner size and reputation are not evidence of fit. A claim that only the rep makes does not count.
- **Absence is a result.** A dimension that the call never touched is Missed. Do not credit it because the call felt friendly.
- **Read the "does not count" line.** Each dimension lists the usual false positives.


## 1. Relationship with buyers

**What it is.** The partner describes how they relate to the customers who would buy.

**Covered:** the partner describes their relationship with buyers in the segment, with a count or a named example. **Partial:** the partner says they have good relationships. **Missed:** no relationship comes up.

**Does not count:** "we are well known". Reputation with no buyer named.

**A strong call** asks for the last customer that came to them for advice, and why.

## 2. Current engagement with vendors

**What it is.** The partner says which vendors they work with and how.

**Covered:** the partner names vendors, the number of deals and what they do for each. **Partial:** the partner says they work with vendors and names none. **Missed:** no vendor work comes up.

**Does not count:** partner size. A vendor that only the rep names.

**A strong call** asks how many deals they closed for each vendor last year.

## 3. Capacity

**What it is.** The partner says who would work on this and how much time they have.

**Covered:** the partner names people and their time, or states a hiring condition. **Partial:** the partner says "my team". **Missed:** no capacity comes up.

**Does not count:** company headcount. A person named with no time stated.

**A strong call** asks who would do the work and how many hours a week.

## 4. Customer reach in the segment

**What it is.** The partner counts the customers who could use the product.

**Covered:** the partner gives a count of customers and the share with the need. **Partial:** the partner says "our clients". **Missed:** no reach comes up.

**Does not count:** total client count with no need stated. A list that only the rep builds.

**A strong call** asks how many of their clients have the problem and which ones.

## 5. Technology habits

**What it is.** The partner says how they adopt new tools.

**Covered:** the partner names the tools they run and how they learned the last new one. **Partial:** the partner says they are comfortable with technology. **Missed:** technology does not come up.

**Does not count:** "we are comfortable learning new systems" with no example.

**A strong call** asks about the last new tool they adopted and how long it took.

## Reads-well-too test

Run this test in both directions before you finalize an audit.

- A call that covered every dimension with the partner's own words must not come back mostly Missed. When it does, widen what counts as evidence.
- A call that never raised a dimension must not get credit for it. When a status rests on a line from a "does not count" list, remove that status.

## Sources

The dimensions follow common channel-management practice. The evidence rules and the strong-call lines come from reviews of recorded partner and sales calls.
