# Partner-Business-Review rubric

This rubric has 7 dimensions. It audits a call with a channel or reseller partner. The partner is the other side of the call, so partner words count the way buyer words count in a sales call.

Three rules apply to every dimension.

- **The partner must say it.** Evidence is something the partner said. A claim that only the vendor's rep makes, such as "they love us", does not count. Partner size and reputation are not evidence of fit. A claim that only the rep makes does not count.
- **Absence is a result.** A dimension that the call never touched is Missed. Do not credit it because the call felt friendly.
- **Read the "does not count" line.** Each dimension lists the usual false positives.


## 1. Results against goals

**What it is.** The partner states the goal and the result for the period.

**Covered:** the partner states a goal and a result in numbers. **Partial:** the partner says "a good quarter". **Missed:** no results come up.

**Does not count:** the rep's own recap. A goal with no result.

**A strong call** asks for the goal, the result, and the difference, in numbers.

## 2. Pipeline with named customers

**What it is.** The partner names the deals in their pipeline and where each stands.

**Covered:** the partner names the customers and the stage of each. **Partial:** the partner says "a few deals". **Missed:** no pipeline comes up.

**Does not count:** a demo or POC alone, which does not make a deal. Pipeline talk with no named customer.

**A strong call** asks for each named customer, the stage, and the next step.

## 3. Registration status as given

**What it is.** The partner states the registration status of each deal.

**Covered:** the partner states a status for each named deal, exactly as they know it. **Partial:** the partner says "I think so". **Missed:** registration does not come up.

**Does not count:** a status that the rep guesses from the stage.

**A strong call** asks for each deal's status and the date it was registered.

## 4. Blockers with owners

**What it is.** The partner names what blocks them and who will fix it.

**Covered:** the partner names a blocker, and an owner and a date are agreed. **Partial:** a blocker is named with no owner or date. **Missed:** no blocker is raised or asked about.

**Does not count:** "I will send the new docs" with no date. A blocker that only the rep names.

**A strong call** asks what blocked the last deal and agrees an owner and a date for each blocker.

## 5. Support needs, specific

**What it is.** The partner states the specific support that they need.

**Covered:** the partner names a specific need, such as training for a named person. **Partial:** the partner names an area, such as "more help". **Missed:** no support need comes up.

**Does not count:** "more enablement". A need that only the rep proposes.

**A strong call** asks what one thing from the vendor would add a deal next quarter.

## 6. Next-period goal, measurable

**What it is.** The partner states a goal for next period in numbers.

**Covered:** the partner states a number and what it rests on. **Partial:** the partner states a hope with no number. **Missed:** no goal comes up.

**Does not count:** "grow". A goal that only the rep sets.

**A strong call** asks for the number and the named deals behind it.

## 7. Next review fixed

**What it is.** A date and attendees for the next review are agreed.

**Covered:** a date and attendees are agreed. **Partial:** a cadence is named with no date. **Missed:** no review is set.

**Does not count:** "in three months" with no date. "We will talk".

**A strong call** books the date and the attendees before the call ends.

## Reads-well-too test

Run this test in both directions before you finalize an audit.

- A call that covered every dimension with the partner's own words must not come back mostly Missed. When it does, widen what counts as evidence.
- A call that never raised a dimension must not get credit for it. When a status rests on a line from a "does not count" list, remove that status.

## Sources

The dimensions follow common channel-management practice. The evidence rules and the strong-call lines come from reviews of recorded partner and sales calls.
