# New-business registration rubric

Six dimensions covering whether a new-logo deal registration is complete
enough to trust, plus a reads-well-too check. Deal registration has no
canonical public framework the way MEDDICC or BANT does — this rubric is
built from general channel/deal-registration practice (duplicate/conflict
checking, source attribution, ICP fit), not a named standard.

## 1. Legal entity + domain identified

Is the registration tied to a specific legal entity and domain, or just a
company name that could match several entities (parent, subsidiary, DBA)?

## 2. Duplicate/conflict check run

Was the account/pipeline checked for an existing owner or open opportunity
before registering, or does the record show no evidence a check happened?

## 3. Source attribution recorded

Is it clear how this opportunity was found — inbound, referral, prospecting,
event, partner-sourced lead — or is the source missing or vague?

## 4. ICP fit stated, not assumed

Is there a stated reason this account fits the ideal customer profile
(segment, size, use case), or is fit just implied by the registration
existing?

## 5. Requested support/resources specified

Does the registration name what's being asked for — deal registration
protection, technical resources, marketing support — or is the ask
unspecified?

## 6. Registration expiry / validity window set

Is there a defined validity window or expiry date on the registration, or
is it open-ended with no review point?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against a registration you know was clean and uncontested. If
it still comes back mostly Missed, loosen the read before reporting.
