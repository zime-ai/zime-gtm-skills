# Next-step-commitment rubric

Three elements, all required for a Real commitment verdict. Missing any one
drops the verdict to Weak; missing all three (or ending with nothing
resembling a next step at all) is No commitment.

## 1. Specific action

Not "follow up," "circle back," or "stay in touch" — a named thing:
a demo scheduled, a proposal sent for review, an intro made to a named
stakeholder, a signed document reviewed by legal.

## 2. Date or concrete trigger

Either an actual calendar date/time, or an event with an obvious, checkable
occurrence ("once procurement responds to the security questionnaire").
"Soon," "next quarter," or "early next year" with nothing more specific
fails this element.

## 3. Two-sided ownership

The prospect commits to doing something too — showing up to a scheduled
call, looping in a stakeholder, reviewing a document by a date — not just
the rep promising to send something. A commitment that is entirely the
rep's to fulfill is not a real next step; it's a to-do item the rep gave
themselves.

## Verdicts

- **Real commitment** — all three present
- **Weak commitment** — one or two present; name exactly which are missing
- **No commitment** — the call ends without anything resembling a next step,
  or with something so vague none of the three elements are present

## Reads-well-too check

If a call that clearly ended in a scheduled, dated, two-sided next step
still gets marked Weak because of an overly literal reading (e.g. the date
was implied by "same time next week" rather than a calendar date), loosen
the reading rather than penalizing a call that plainly closed out well.
