# SQL-to-qualify rubric

Five dimensions that separate a real, worth-pursuing opportunity from an SQL
that's just carrying a form-fill label. This is the gate before
`meeting-to-qualify`'s deeper authority/competition/next-step pass — it asks
whether there's anything here worth that deeper pass at all.

## 1. Contact reachability and role relevance

Is the person on the call someone with a real stake in the problem, or a
clear path to that person — or did the rep end up talking to whoever
answered the inbound form, with no line to anyone who matters? A call with
the wrong person and no bridge to the right one is a Missed regardless of
how the rest of the conversation went.

## 2. Pain restated in the prospect's own words

Did the prospect describe the problem in their own language on the call, or
is the rep (and this audit) still leaning on the MQL form's checkbox
answers? A prospect who can't restate why they filled out the form is a
common false-positive — the pain was assumed, not confirmed.

## 3. Budget/urgency plausibility

Not a confirmed number or a locked timeline — a rough fit check. Did
anything on the call suggest this account could plausibly fund a purchase
at a realistic price point, and is there any real reason to move now versus
someday? Pure silence on both is a Partial at best.

## 4. Competitive awareness surfaced

Did the call surface whether the prospect is already looking at
alternatives (competitors, an internal build, doing nothing), or did the
rep never ask? Zero competitive context this early isn't automatically bad
— it can mean they aren't shopping seriously yet — but the call should have
at least asked, not left it unknown.

## 5. Concrete next meeting booked

Did the call end with a specific next meeting, a date, and the right
attendee(s) named — or a vague "I'll follow up"? No booked next step is the
clearest single tell that this SQL isn't converting to a worked opportunity
yet.

## Reads-well-too check

Before finalizing, sanity-check against a call you already know was a
strong, real SQL. If it still comes back mostly Missed, the read is tuned
too strictly — loosen what counts as evidence before reporting.
