# Qualification-call rubric

Eight dimensions that separate a real, worth-pursuing deal from one that
looks active but isn't. This is a go/no-go lens, not a deep-discovery pass —
shallower on pain exploration, sharper on whether the deal itself holds up.

## 1. Problem confirmed as real and urgent

Did the prospect confirm this is an active priority with a reason it needs
solving now, not a "sounds interesting, send me something" response? Vague
interest without urgency is the single most common false-positive in a
qualified pipeline.

## 2. Buying authority mapped

Is the person on the call someone who can say yes, or who clearly named who
can? A qualification call that ends without knowing this is incomplete
regardless of how good the rest of the conversation was.

## 3. Budget plausibility

Not a confirmed number — a plausibility check. Did anything on the call
suggest this prospect could actually fund a purchase at a realistic price
point, or is that entirely unknown?

## 4. Timeline reality

Is there a specific event, deadline, or process driving a decision by a
particular point, or only an open-ended "sometime this year"? The latter is
a Partial at best.

## 5. Fit with what's being sold

Does the prospect's current tooling and stated need actually line up with
what's being offered, or is there a mismatch the rep glossed over to keep
the deal moving?

## 6. Competitive situation

Is the prospect evaluating this seriously against alternatives, or are they
just taking a meeting? A prospect with zero awareness of alternatives this
early is sometimes a sign they aren't seriously shopping yet.

## 7. Engagement as a signal

Genuine two-way engagement, or a prospect being polite while giving minimal
information? Low engagement at the qualification stage is a stronger
warning sign than at discovery, because there's been more chance to invest.

## 8. Concrete next step secured

Did the call end with a specific next step both sides agreed to — a date, a
person to loop in, a document to review — or a vague "let's stay in touch"?
No concrete next step is often the clearest single tell that the deal isn't
real yet.

## Reads-well-too check

Before finalizing, sanity-check against a call you already know was a
strong, real deal. If it still comes back mostly Missed, the read is
tuned too strictly — loosen what counts as evidence before reporting.
