# WBR-Report-Generator rules

Reads one or two exports. Every figure comes from the rows and is shown with how it was computed. The report states what the data cannot tell, so a leader does not read a cause into a number.

Three rules apply to every section.

- **Cite or leave it out.** Every claim traces to a quote, a timestamp, or a column and cell. A claim with no cited source does not ship.
- **The buyer or customer must say it.** A claim that only the rep makes does not count unless the other side accepted it. Silence is not a yes.
- **Unknown stays unknown.** When the input does not say it, write "not stated". Do not fill the gap with a guess or with outside knowledge.

## Headline numbers

State total open pipeline, the commit total, the number of open deals, and the average open deal size. Show each figure with the rows it came from. State closed-won and closed-lost figures only when closed rows exist.

## Movement

With a previous export, list deals that are new, advanced, slipped (a later close date), or left, matched by deal name. Without one, write "no previous export". Never infer movement.

## Risks to next period

List at most four risks that cells support, such as commit deals with a past close date, deals with no next step, or a large amount in one stage. Cite the cells.

## What the export cannot tell

State what the data cannot answer, such as why a deal slipped or how a rep feels about a deal. List at most three items.

## Asks for the meeting

Write up to three asks for the leaders, each tied to a risk or gap above.

## What does not count

- A cause for a number that the export does not hold.
- A trend from one export.
- A rate computed from fewer than five closed rows, stated without that caveat.
- A figure with no stated source rows.
- A narrative sentence that praises or blames a rep.

## Reads-well-too test

Run this test in both directions before you finalize.

- A quiet week with no movement and no flags must produce a short report that says so. Do not manufacture a story.
- A thin input must not get a padded output. When the input supports two items, write two. Do not invent a third to reach a count.

## Sources

The structure follows common weekly business review practice. The rules come from reviews of exports.
