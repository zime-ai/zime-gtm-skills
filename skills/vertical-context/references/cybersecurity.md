**Unreviewed** — see `SKILL.md`'s "Status: unreviewed" note.

## Typical buyer titles / who signs

CISO or VP Security owns the technical decision; for mid-market accounts a
Security Manager or Director of IT often runs point day-to-day, with the
CISO or a CFO/COO as final approver above a threshold. In smaller companies
"IT Director" may hold both the technical and budget decision.

## Compliance / regulatory drivers

SOC 2 (Type II renewal cycles are a recurring urgency trigger), cyber
insurance renewals (increasingly ask about MTTR and detection coverage),
board-level incident-reporting requirements, and sector-specific overlays
(PCI-DSS for anyone touching payment data, HIPAA if handling health data
adjacently).

## Specialized pain categories (variant of the generic five buckets)

1. **Tooling gaps and alert fatigue** — fragmented tools, manual triage,
   analyst burnout
2. **Threat exposure / incident history** — near-misses, actual breaches,
   detection-speed doubts
3. **Compliance and audit pressure** — insurance renewals, SOC 2/PCI/HIPAA
   asks
4. **Budget and headcount constraints** — can't get analyst headcount
   approved, "we already bought a tool for that"
5. **Exec/board pressure** — a board question or exec directive is driving
   the evaluation, not just the security team's own initiative

## Domain vocabulary

MTTR, SOC (security operations center), triage, lateral movement, false
positive, EDR/XDR, alert volume, incident response, detection coverage,
credential compromise, security review (as a procurement gate).

## Procurement shape

Security tooling purchases commonly route through a security review even
when the buyer is outside the security team — this can add weeks to a
timeline that looked otherwise ready to close. Ask early whether "anything
touching our stack" triggers this review.

## Disqualifying signal

The prospect frames every pain as "the tool we already bought will handle
that" — a sign leadership sees security spend as a solved, one-time
purchase rather than an ongoing operational gap.
