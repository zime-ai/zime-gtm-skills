# Copy patterns

Observed identically in both source decks — follow these when writing any
new client-specific line, and never rewrite the fixed boilerplate lines to
"fit the client":

- **Second person, their word for it.** "Your best practices", "your winning
  behaviors" — never "the client's" or third person.
- **Champion's goal is quoted, not paraphrased.** "I have 75 accounts and I
  want to scale to 150 by year end" — keep the actual sentence structure and
  numbers from the transcript.
- **One number per risk tile.** A stat + a one-line consequence, never a
  paragraph. If you can't compress a risk to one stat and one sentence, it's
  not ready for a tile — hold it for the notes/appendix instead.
- **Chip grids over paragraphs** for anything catalogue-like (initiatives,
  needs) — short noun phrases, not sentences.
- **Blockers are named as a missing system, not a person's fault.** "No
  shared standard, judgment lives with a couple of senior reps" — not "reps
  aren't following process."
- **Never touch the fixed boilerplate copy** — the two-options slide,
  partnership split, Zime pricing, build-vs-buy, and why-not-Claude slides
  are word-for-word identical across both sample decks. Rewriting them "for
  this client" breaks the one thing keeping the deck fast to produce.
