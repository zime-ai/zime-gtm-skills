# Demo-call rubric

Seven dimensions covering whether a demo was tailored, well-attended, and
landed with a clear next step — not a checklist of features shown.

## 1. Tailored to stated priorities

Did the demo walk through what this specific prospect said mattered
earlier, or was it a generic tour of the product regardless of what came up
in discovery?

## 2. Right stakeholders present

Were the people who actually need to be convinced in the room — technical
evaluators, economic buyer, end users — or just the same one or two people
from earlier calls?

## 3. Engagement during the demo

Did the prospect ask questions, react to specific features, or push back on
anything — or sit largely silent through the whole thing? Silence during a
demo is a weaker signal than it looks.

## 4. Competitive differentiation addressed

If the prospect is evaluating alternatives, did the demo address why this
is different or better, or did it just show the product in a vacuum?

## 5. Fit against current tooling

Did the demo connect back to what the prospect uses today — what it
replaces, what it integrates with — or float disconnected from their actual
environment?

## 6. Success criteria reconfirmed

Did the demo circle back to the outcome the prospect said they wanted, and
show it, specifically — or just show capabilities and hope they land?

## 7. Clear outcome and next step

Did the call end with a specific next step both sides agreed to, or a
vague "we'll follow up"?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against a demo you know landed well. If it still comes back
mostly Missed, loosen the read before reporting.
