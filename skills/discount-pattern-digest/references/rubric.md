# Discount-Pattern-Digest rules

Reads one export with a discount column and an owner column. It describes the book of business. It does not judge whether a discount was right, and it does not link discounts to win or loss.

Three rules apply to every section.

- **Cite or leave it out.** Every claim traces to a quote, a timestamp, or a column and cell. A claim with no cited source does not ship.
- **The buyer or customer must say it.** A claim that only the rep makes does not count unless the other side accepted it. Silence is not a yes.
- **Unknown stays unknown.** When the input does not say it, write "not stated". Do not fill the gap with a guess or with outside knowledge.

## Overall

State the median discount and the number of deals, computed from the rows.

## By owner

For each owner with at least three deals, state the number of deals and the median discount. Name an owner as a pattern only when that median is 10 points or more above the overall median and at least three deals support it. Owners with fewer than three deals are listed as too few to read. Apply the rule to all of an owner's deals. Do not drop outliers first to test whether the pattern survives.

## Outliers

List each deal whose discount is 10 points or more above the overall median. Show owner, stage, amount, and discount. Cite the cells. Rank by discount.

## What it does not show

State once that the digest does not link a discount to a win, a loss, or a reason, because the export does not hold that.

## Questions for the leader

Write one question per pattern or per cluster of outliers that a leader can ask the owner.

## What does not count

- A pattern that rests on one or two deals.
- Removing an owner's highest deals before testing the pattern.
- A reason for a discount that the export does not state.
- A link between discounts and wins or losses.
- A comparison between owners with fewer than three deals.
- An outlier listed with no cited cell.

## Reads-well-too test

Run this test in both directions before you finalize.

- An export where no owner is 10 points above the overall median and no deal is an outlier must produce "no pattern and no outlier". Do not invent one.
- A thin input must not get a padded output. When the input supports two items, write two. Do not invent a third to reach a count.

## Sources

The digest follows common discount-governance practice. The rules come from reviews of exports.
