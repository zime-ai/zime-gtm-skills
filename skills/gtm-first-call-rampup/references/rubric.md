# First-call rampup rubric

Six foundational techniques for a rep still early in ramp. This is a
coaching lens — the point is building the skill, not judging the deal.

## 1. Strong opening

Did the rep open with a clear agenda and a reason the prospect should stay
engaged, or launch straight into questions or a pitch with no framing?

## 2. Open-ended questioning

Are the rep's questions genuinely open ("tell me about...", "walk me
through...") or mostly closed/leading ones that invite a yes/no and stop
the conversation?

## 3. Pain & priority uncovered

Did the rep get past a surface answer to something the prospect actually
described as a real problem, or accept the first vague response and move
on?

## 4. Trigger surfaced

Did the rep ask, in some form, why this is being looked at now rather than
earlier or later?

## 5. Pitch clarity

When the rep did describe the product, was it specific and tied to
something the prospect said, or a generic recitation of features?

## 6. Rapport

Does the conversation read as genuine back-and-forth, or the rep working
through a script while the prospect gives short answers?

## Reads-well-too check

Before finalizing, consider whether this read would feel fair and
motivating to the rep, not just accurate. A new rep who did several things
well and one thing not-yet-there should hear that ratio reflected in the
output, not a wall of Missed items.
