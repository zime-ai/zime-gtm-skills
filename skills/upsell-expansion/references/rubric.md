# Upsell & expansion rubric

Eight dimensions for an expansion or upsell conversation. The account's
underlying health comes first — an expansion pushed onto a wobbling account
is a bigger risk than any gap in the pitch itself.

## 1. Account health check-in first

Before anything else: does this call surface any renewal risk, unresolved
product issue, or fading champion? If yes, flag it plainly — an expansion
conversation on an unhealthy account should slow down, not speed up.

## 2. The expansion ask is concrete

Is there a specific use case, team, or product line driving the expansion
ask, or is it a generic "you should buy more"?

## 3. Champion strength for this ask

Does the internal champion have the standing and motivation to actually
advocate for this expansion internally, not just be personally interested
in it?

## 4. Confidence read

Based on the conversation, how likely does this expansion feel to actually
close — and is that read grounded in something specific from the call, or
just general optimism?

## 5. Strategic alignment

Does this expansion tie into something the customer's leadership is
actively prioritizing this year, or is it opportunistic with no larger
story behind it?

## 6. Open risks or product issues named

Did any unresolved product issue or open complaint surface that should be
addressed before or alongside the expansion ask?

## 7. Enablement gap check

If usage of the existing product is uneven, is that because of a training
gap (fixable, and maybe part of the expansion pitch) or a fit problem
(a reason to pause, not expand)?

## 8. Clear next step

Did the call end with a specific next step — a proposal date, a
stakeholder to loop in — or general interest with nothing scheduled?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against an expansion call you know led to a healthy close. If
it still comes back mostly Missed, loosen the read before reporting — but
keep dimension 1 strict either way: when genuinely unsure whether an
account is healthy enough to expand, say so rather than defaulting to
"probably fine."
