# Qualify-to-POC rubric

Five dimensions covering whether a qualified deal is actually ready to
start a POC/pilot — the go/no-go gate before pilot work begins, not a
read on how the pilot itself is going.

## 1. Success criteria defined and agreed

Is there a specific, measurable definition of what "success" looks like
for the POC, and did both sides explicitly sign off on it — or is the
pilot starting with only a vague sense of what it needs to prove?

## 2. Technical scope agreed

Is it clear what's in scope and out of scope for the POC — which systems
it integrates with, which use cases it covers, which it doesn't — or is
the scope still fuzzy enough that it could expand or drift once the pilot
starts?

## 3. Stakeholders identified and committed

Is it clear who on the customer side will actually evaluate the pilot day
to day, and who has the authority to sign off on the result at the end —
or does the deal still lack a named owner and a named decision-maker?

## 4. POC timeline and exit criteria set

Are there agreed start and end dates, and a clear definition of what
"pass" looks like at the end — or is the pilot open-ended with no fixed
point where it gets judged?

## 5. Resourcing confirmed

Is there a named person (or people) on the customer side who will
actually do the work the pilot requires — configuration, testing,
feedback — or is that work assumed to happen without anyone committed to
it?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against a POC kickoff you know was well-run. If it still
comes back mostly Missed, loosen the read before reporting.
