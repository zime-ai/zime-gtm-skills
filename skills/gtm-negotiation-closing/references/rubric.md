# Negotiation & closing rubric

Six dimensions covering whether a late-stage deal is genuinely moving
toward signature, not just labeled "closing soon."

## 1. Reaction to pricing

How did the prospect actually react to pricing — pushback, silence,
enthusiasm — and is that reaction being taken at face value or explained
away?

## 2. Procurement process is moving

Is the deal actively moving through the prospect's procurement process, or
sitting with no visible movement since the last update?

## 3. Contracting awareness

Does the prospect understand what contracting actually involves on their
side — legal review, security questionnaire, signature authority — or is
this being discovered late?

## 4. Closure timeline specificity

Is there a specific date or event driving close, or a vague "soon" that's
moved before?

## 5. Budget confirmed at this stage

Has budget been explicitly confirmed for this specific proposal, not just
assumed plausible from earlier qualification?

## 6. Clear path to signature

Did the call produce a specific next action toward signature — a redline
turnaround, a specific approver's sign-off, a signing date — or general
optimism with nothing concrete?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against a negotiation call you know led to a fast, clean
close. If it still comes back mostly Missed, loosen the read before
reporting.
