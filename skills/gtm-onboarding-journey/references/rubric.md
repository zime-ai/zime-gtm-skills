# Onboarding-journey rubric

Five dimensions for a new-customer onboarding check-in. Kept short
deliberately — onboarding calls are shorter and more procedural than a
sales call, and a longer rubric here would be inventing signal that isn't
there.

## 1. Onboarding experience quality

Is setup going smoothly from the customer's side, or are they still
fighting basic configuration issues past when that should be resolved?

## 2. Customer sentiment

What's the actual tone — genuinely positive about how things are going, or
polite-but-frustrated? Note when the words and the tone disagree.

## 3. Early engagement signal

Is the customer actively participating — asking questions, testing things
themselves — or passively waiting to be told what to do next?

## 4. Milestone / agenda clarity

Is there a clear next milestone both sides agree on, or is onboarding
drifting with no defined finish line?

## 5. Early expansion or add-on signal

Did anything come up — an adjacent use case, another team's interest — that
CS should flag for later, even if it's not the focus of this call?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against an onboarding you know went smoothly. If it still
reads as needs-attention, loosen the read before reporting.
