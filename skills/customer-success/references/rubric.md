# Customer success / QBR rubric

Eight dimensions for a periodic health review — a broader lens than a
risk-triggered or opportunity-triggered conversation. The point is an
honest snapshot of the relationship, not a status-update formality.

## 1. Value delivered against original goals

Can the customer connect current usage back to what they originally set out
to achieve, in specific terms — or has the original goal been forgotten
and the conversation drifted into generic status updates?

## 2. Usage breadth across the account

Is usage spread across the team or account, or still concentrated in one or
two people regardless of how positive the tone is? Breadth matters more
here than depth on any one workflow.

## 3. Open issues resolved, not just raised

For any issue that came up in a prior review: was it actually closed out,
or raised again with no update? A recurring unresolved issue is a stronger
signal than a new one.

## 4. Enablement gaps

Is there a training or adoption gap holding back value, separate from
whether the product itself fits the need?

## 5. Stakeholder breadth

Is this conversation still with the same single contact from the original
deal, or has the relationship broadened to other stakeholders? A single
point of contact is a standing risk even absent any other bad signal.

## 6. Strategic alignment

Does this account connect to something the customer's leadership actively
cares about this year, or has it become "a tool we have" with no strategic
story?

## 7. Forward-looking fit

Did the conversation surface anything relevant to what's next for this
account — a new use case, a roadmap need — without it being a sales push?

## 8. Clear cadence for next check-in

Did the call end with an agreed date or trigger for the next review, or
does it drift until something goes wrong?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against a QBR you know went well. If it still comes back
mostly Missed, loosen the read before reporting.
