# End-client registration rubric

Seven dimensions covering whether a partner's end-client deal registration
is complete and clean enough to accept into the partner program's pipeline
— not whether the underlying deal itself is any good.

**No canonical framework caveat.** Deal/channel registration has no
canonical public framework the way BANT or MEDDPICC do. These seven
dimensions are built from general partner-program practice (conflict
checking against the direct sales pipeline, permission to engage, keeping
the partner and the end client as distinct entities), not from any named
standard — don't imply one exists.

## 1. Partner identity confirmed

Is it clear which partner organization is submitting the registration —
name, and the specific partner contact standing behind the request — or is
the registration ambiguous about who is actually registering the deal?

## 2. End-client entity identified distinctly from the partner

Is the end client named as its own entity, separate from the partner? A
registration that conflates the two (e.g. the partner's own name used as
the end-client field, or the end client left generic) can't be evaluated
for conflict or permission at all.

## 3. Engagement scope stated

Is it stated what the partner is actually being credited for — sourcing
the lead, technical implementation, ongoing management, a referral only —
or is the scope vague enough that a later dispute over partner credit is
inevitable?

## 4. Conflict check run against direct/house pipeline

Was the end client actually checked against the direct sales pipeline for
an existing or recent house-owned opportunity before the registration was
accepted, or was it accepted without that check ever happening?

## 5. End-client contact named, with confirmed permission to engage

Is there a named contact at the end client, and is there confirmation that
contact has agreed to be engaged by the partner on this deal — or is the
partner claiming a relationship without anything to back it up?

## 6. Commercial terms recorded

Are the commercial terms — margin or discount tier, and whether this is a
referral or a resell arrangement — actually recorded on the registration,
or left blank for later?

## 7. Registration expiry / validity window set

Does the registration have a defined validity window (an expiry or
protection-period date) after which it lapses if no progress is made, or is
it open-ended with no forcing function?

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against a registration you know was clean and uncontested. If
it still comes back mostly Missed, loosen the read before reporting.
