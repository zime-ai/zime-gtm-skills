# End-client registration rubric

Seven dimensions covering whether a partner's end-client deal registration
is complete and clean enough to accept into the partner program's pipeline
— not whether the underlying deal itself is any good.

**No canonical framework caveat.** Deal/channel registration has no
canonical public framework the way BANT or MEDDPICC do. These seven
dimensions are built from general partner-program practice (conflict
checking against the direct sales pipeline, permission to engage, keeping
the partner and the end client as distinct entities), not from any named
standard — don't imply one exists.

## 1. Partner identity confirmed

**What it is.** Which partner organization is submitting the registration
— name and the specific partner contact behind the request — is
unambiguous.

**Covered vs. a miss.** Covered looks like a named partner organization and
contact. A miss looks like the registration ambiguous about who is
actually registering the deal.

**Weak:** "Submitted via our partner portal." **Strong:** "TechReseller
Inc., submitted by their channel manager Jane Ortiz."

## 2. End-client entity identified distinctly from the partner

**What it is.** The end client is named as its own entity, separate from
the partner — not conflated with it.

**Covered vs. a miss.** Covered looks like a distinct end-client name and
domain. A miss looks like the partner's own name reused as the end-client
field, or the end client left generic.

**Weak:** "End client: TechReseller's customer." **Strong:** "End client:
Meridian Logistics, domain meridianlog.com."

## 3. Engagement scope stated

**What it is.** What the partner is actually being credited for — sourcing
the lead, technical implementation, ongoing management, a referral only —
is stated plainly.

**Covered vs. a miss.** Covered looks like a specific credited role named.
A miss looks like the scope vague enough that a later dispute over partner
credit is inevitable.

**Weak:** "Partner is involved in the deal." **Strong:** "Partner sourced
the lead and will own implementation; Zime team handles the commercial
close."

## 4. Conflict check run against direct/house pipeline

**What it is.** The end client was actually checked against the direct
sales pipeline for an existing or recent house-owned opportunity before the
registration was accepted.

**Covered vs. a miss.** Covered looks like a stated check result. A miss
looks like the registration accepted without that check ever happening.

**Weak:** "Probably not in our pipeline." **Strong:** "Checked against
house pipeline — no existing or recent opportunity found for Meridian
Logistics."

## 5. End-client contact named, with confirmed permission to engage

**What it is.** A named contact at the end client, with confirmation that
contact has agreed to be engaged by the partner on this deal.

**Covered vs. a miss.** Covered looks like a named contact plus explicit
confirmation of engagement permission. A miss looks like the partner
claiming a relationship with nothing to back it up.

**Weak:** "Partner says they know the buyer." **Strong:** "Contact: Raj
Mehta, VP Ops at Meridian, who confirmed by email he's expecting the
partner's outreach."

## 6. Commercial terms recorded

**What it is.** The commercial terms — margin or discount tier, and
whether this is a referral or a resell arrangement — actually recorded on
the registration.

**Covered vs. a miss.** Covered looks like a specific tier and arrangement
type stated. A miss looks like terms left blank for later.

**Weak:** "Terms TBD." **Strong:** "15% referral margin, resell
arrangement, per the Tier 2 partner agreement."

## 7. Registration expiry / validity window set

**What it is.** A defined validity window (an expiry or protection-period
date) after which the registration lapses if no progress is made.

**Covered vs. a miss.** Covered looks like a specific expiry date. A miss
looks like the registration open-ended with no forcing function.

**Weak:** "No expiry needed." **Strong:** "90-day protection window from
registration date, auto-reviewed if no stage movement."

## Reads-well-too check

Sanity-check against a registration you know was clean and uncontested. If
it still comes back mostly Missed, loosen the read before reporting.
